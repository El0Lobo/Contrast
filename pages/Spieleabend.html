Games Night at Contrast

Games Night at Contrast is a highlight for everyone who loves board games, card games or party games. Whether you prefer classics or modern games, there is something for everyone!

Enjoy a relaxed and entertaining atmosphere, meet new people and bring your favourite games. A wide selection of games is also available at the venue.

Games Night usually takes place on the first weekend of each month at Contrast and is an ideal opportunity to enjoy an evening full of fun and community. Whether you are a casual player or a passionate strategist, you are welcome here.

We invite you to join an open community of games enthusiasts. Whether you simply want to unwind or plan an epic games night, anything is possible at Contrast!

To keep up with upcoming Games Nights, follow our Instagram page. You will find all the latest dates and highlights there!
