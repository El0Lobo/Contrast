Das Labor

The Lab is our experimental night, held every Tuesday at Contrast. Electronic sounds meet experimental music and creative performances. Immerse yourself in a night of innovative sounds and discover new musical landscapes that invite you to dream, dance and reflect.

The Lab gives artists and DJs the opportunity to push their boundaries and thrill the audience with distinctive performances. It is the perfect night for music lovers in search of the extraordinary.

Keep up with our next events and highlights by following our Instagram page. You will find all updates on upcoming performances, themed nights and exclusive sessions there.
