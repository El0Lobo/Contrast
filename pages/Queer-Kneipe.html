Celebrate diversity at Queer Night

Queer Night at Contrast is a safer space for the queer community and everyone who appreciates an open and inclusive atmosphere. Spend your Monday evening here with good music, friendly conversations and a vibrant community.

Our goal is to foster acceptance, diversity and solidarity, regardless of gender, sexuality or background. Queer Night is a place where everyone is welcome.

Queer Night takes place every Monday and offers a relaxed setting to start the week with friends. Its colourful atmosphere invites you to become part of our community.

Music, conversation and a strong sense of community take centre stage. Queer Night is a place where you can be yourself and connect with like-minded people.
