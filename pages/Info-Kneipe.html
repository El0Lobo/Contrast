Info Night

Info Night at Contrast brings knowledge and community together. It is all about engaging talks, discussions and conversations about the current issues that matter to us.

From social and political questions to environmental issues, sustainability and cultural highlights, Info Night offers a platform for anyone who is curious and wants to learn more.

We regularly invite experts, activists and guests to share insights, broaden perspectives and inspire reflection. As always, there are affordable drinks and an open, welcoming atmosphere.

Keep up with upcoming events and themed nights by following our Instagram page.
