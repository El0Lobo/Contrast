Since 1985 · Konstanz

A room for what does not fit elsewhere.

Contrast is a self-managed youth club where subculture, political exchange, art and loud music meet.

How we work

Not a brand. A collective.

Run by Jugendkultur e.V., Contrast is made by the people who use it. We organise concerts, DJ nights, talks, workshops and regular open evenings—not for profit, but because a city needs places where culture can stay rough around the edges.

Graffiti on the walls, affordable drinks at the bar, table football in the corner: everything here is an invitation to arrive as you are and take part.

What matters here

Solidarity is the programme.

Safer spaceSexism, racism, homophobia, transphobia, fascism and every other form of discrimination have no place here.

AccessCulture should be possible without a big budget. We keep entry and drinks as accessible as we can.

ParticipationHave an idea, want to play, exhibit, organise or help out? Contrast is shaped by people who get involved.

Mauerblümchen → Outback → Contrast

Three names. One place.

As Mauerblümchen, this was a self-organised space in Chérisy for loud music, exchange and culture beyond commercial clubs. It later became Outback.

Today, Contrast is a non-profit, self-managed youth culture association carried by its members. They organise culture voluntarily and shape the programme together.

The band archive contains 308 entries from Mauerblümchen, Outback and Contrast, including Knochenfabrik · Muff Potter · Jaya the Cat · The Toasters · ZSK · Jay Reatard.
