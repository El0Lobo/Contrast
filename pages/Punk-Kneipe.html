Punk Night

“Punk’s not dead, oh no it’s not!” – The Exploited
Especially not on Thursdays at Contrast.

Whether you are into classics like The Exploited, ZSK or Terrorgruppe, or prefer newer punk: there is pretty much always something playing that you can mosh to, sing along with, shout along to, or simply enjoy.

There is just one important thing: we do not want any racist, sexist, ableist, queerphobic, or otherwise hateful bullshit in the venue.

So if you are up for a night of punk, a relaxed atmosphere and affordable drinks, come by, grab a beer, listen to music, play a round of table football and see where the night takes you.

You are at Contrast on Thursdays, because Thursdays are for Contrast.
