Punk Night

Punk Night at Contrast is the meeting place for everyone who loves rebellious music and an unconventional atmosphere. Every Thursday, Contrast becomes a place full of energy, passion and lively conversations—a night devoted entirely to punk.

Lose yourself in high-energy music, meet like-minded people and enjoy the freedom of the punk spirit. From classics to modern punk bands, the playlist is guaranteed to set the mood.

Contrast is known for its close-knit and open atmosphere. Come by, grab a drink and be part of a community that celebrates being different.
