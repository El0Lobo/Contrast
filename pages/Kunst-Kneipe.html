Get Creative @ Art Night

A unique meeting place for art lovers and creative minds in Konstanz. Art Night at Contrast offers room for inspiration, exchange and creativity, free from commercial pressure.

Let your imagination run wild and dive into a world of art and culture!

Art Night is a special event at Contrast dedicated to supporting art and culture. Local artists, creative minds and anyone interested come together here to experience and create art.

Our focus is on solidarity, acceptance and creative expression. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced artist, everyone is welcome.

Our venue is a living canvas that reflects the energy and creative spirit of our community. Come by and be inspired!

Stay up to date and learn more about our events and activities on our Instagram page.
