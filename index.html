About us

Contrast is a youth club in Konstanz run by Jugendkultur e.V. Known for its vibrant subculture atmosphere, it offers a colourful mix of punk, techno and alternative events.

Covered in graffiti, with a welcoming atmosphere, affordable drinks and free table football.

Solidarity and acceptance are especially important at Contrast. Our club is a safer space for everyone, regardless of gender, background or interests. We believe in the importance of community and that everyone is welcome and has a voice.

Our walls reflect the creative energy and community spirit of the place. Come by and become part of an open and diverse community that welcomes everyone. We look forward to seeing you at one of our upcoming events!

For more information and current events, visit our Facebook page or our Instagram.
